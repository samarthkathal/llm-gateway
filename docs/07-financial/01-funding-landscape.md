# Funding Landscape & Comparables

## Macro climate (2025–2026)

- AI captured ~50% of global VC in 2025 ($202B into AI); AI startups take ~33% of total VC
  in 2026 ([Qubit Capital trends](https://qubit.capital/blog/ai-startup-fundraising-trends)).
- Median rounds: seed ~$5.75M, Series A ~$18.4M, Series B ~$80M; AI-tagged seeds carry a
  ~42% valuation premium ([Eqvista analysis](https://eqvista.com/ai-startup-fundraising-trends/)).
- Investor framing shifted in our favor: AI governance is now pitched (and increasingly
  bought) as *adoption-enabling* spend rather than defensive cost
  ([New Market Pitch governance ranking](https://newmarketpitch.com/blogs/news/ai-governance-top-startups-fundraising)).
- Concentration warning: top-10 AI-governance startups hold ~52% of the ~$1.5B+ identified
  category funding — followers raise on differentiation, not category membership.
- Cybersecurity capital is consolidating into fewer, larger checks with AI-security
  expanding ([Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/02/25/cybersecurity-venture-funding-ai-security-expands/)).

| Credo AI / Holistic AI | Governance platforms | $40–90M range each | Independent; enterprise traction via regulation |

**Read of the pattern:** capture-layer companies exit early (14–24 months post-A) into
security platforms at solid-but-not-spectacular outcomes. Data/insight-layer positioning is
the argument for a larger standalone outcome — and the honest acknowledgment that a
security-platform acquisition is the modal exit if the bigger bet stalls.

## Fundraising plan (matched to the metrics in [`../04-effort/02-go-to-market-effort.md`](../04-effort/02-go-to-market-effort.md))

### Now → Month 6: pre-seed / bootstrap — $500k–1M
- Basis: thesis + working MVP + 3–5 design partners (LOIs before the raise, not after).
- Sources: angels from security/HR-tech operator networks, pre-seed funds, possibly an
  enterprise design partner's venture arm.
- Spend: 2–3 people, infra, first counsel review. 12–15 months runway.

### Month 9–15: seed — $3–5M
- Entry evidence required: $300–500k ARR signed, 8+ tenants, one referenceable double-story
  case, SOC 2 Type I done, insight attach ≥40%.
- Lead profile: enterprise-SaaS seed funds with AI-security or HR-tech theses (Next47-type
  strategics validated Harmonic's neighborhood).
- Spend: 6–8 people (2 GTM), EU-prep track (residency, works-council pack, ISO start).

### Month 20–30: Series A — $12–18M
- Evidence: $1.5–2.5M ARR, net revenue retention >120% (insight-tier expansion is the NRR
  engine), first EU lighthouse, EU-AI-Act-countdown demand visible in pipeline.
- Narrative: category leadership of "intent intelligence" atop AI-usage governance.

### Non-dilutive / alternatives
- Cloud credits (AWS/GCP/Azure AI programs) offset extraction-compute burn.
- EU: EIC/EIT or national grants plausibly fund the compliance-heavy EU productization.
- Revenue-first discipline: governance-tier deals are cash-early (annual prepay standard in
  security procurement).

## What kills fundability (mirror of the risk register)

Bossware categorization in press or diligence; a tenant privacy incident; Microsoft
shipping org-joined Copilot analytics before we own a differentiated dataset; insight
attach-rate failing (reduces us to a commoditizing gateway pitch).
